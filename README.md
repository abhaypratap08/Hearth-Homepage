# 🏠 Hearth
just a lil custom new-tab page i built because i got tired of every browser homepage extension either being bloated, ugly, or trying to sell me something. nothing fancy, no build step, no framework — just a single HTML file doing its best.

not a serious project. mostly an excuse to rice my browser and procrastinate on actual work.

## what it does

- ⏰ **clock + date** — big minimal clock, click the date for a little calendar popover
- 🔍 **search bar** — glassy pill, hits google on enter
- 🧩 **shortcuts** — add/edit/remove/drag-to-reorder site tiles, auto-grabs favicons or upload your own custom icon
- 🖼️ **custom background** — drop in any image or video, stored locally
- 🎨 **accent color** — default, pulled from your wallpaper's dominant color, or pick your own — recolors the heatmaps too
- 🐙 **GitHub widget** — contribution heatmap + current/best streak, just needs your username
- 💻 **LeetCode widget** — activity heatmap, streaks, and easy/medium/hard solved bars
- 🍅 **pomodoro timer** — focus/short break/long break, tucked in the corner
- 🖱️ **arrange mode** — drag the widgets wherever you want them and lock it in
- 💾 everything lives in `localStorage` — no backend, no accounts, no tracking

## why

mostly just here so I don't lose it. everything lives in `localStorage`, so the second my OS gets wiped (which, let's be honest, is a when not an if) this thing is gone forever unless it's backed up somewhere. this repo is that backup.
